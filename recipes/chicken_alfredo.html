Chicken Alfredo

Description

Chicken Alfredo is a creamy pasta dish made with tender chicken breast, fettuccine pasta, and a rich Alfredo sauce.
The sauce is prepared with butter, cream, and Parmesan cheese, giving the dish a smooth and indulgent flavor.

Ingredients

  • Fettuccine pasta
  • Chicken breast
  • Heavy cream
  • Butter
  • Garlic
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Olive oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Fresh parsley (optional)

Steps

  1. Cook fettuccine in salted boiling water and drain.
  2. Season chicken with salt and pepper, then cook in olive oil until golden and fully cooked. Slice and set aside.
  3. In a pan, melt butter, add garlic, then stir in cream and simmer gently.
  4. Add Parmesan cheese and stir until the sauce thickens.
  5. Combine pasta, chicken, and sauce. Garnish with parsley and serve.
