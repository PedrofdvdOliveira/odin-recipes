Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian baked pasta dish made by layering wide pasta sheets with rich meat or vegetable sauce, creamy béchamel or ricotta,
and melted cheese, usually mozzarella and Parmesan. The sauce is often a slow-simmered tomato-based ragù with ground beef or sausage,
though vegetarian versions with vegetables like spinach, zucchini, or mushrooms are also popular.

The layered dish is baked until bubbly and golden on top, allowing the flavors to meld and the pasta to become tender.
Lasagna is known for its hearty, comforting taste and is commonly served as a main course for family meals or special gatherings.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna pasta sheets
  • Ground beef or Italian sausage
  • Tomato sauce or crushed tomatoes
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Olive oil
  • Ricotta cheese or béchamel sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Dried oregano or Italian seasoning
  • Fresh basil (optional)

Steps

  1. Prepare the sauce: Heat olive oil in a pan, sauté chopped onion and garlic, then add ground meat and cook until browned.
    Stir in tomato sauce, salt, pepper, and herbs, and let it simmer for 15–30 minutes.
  2. Cook the pasta: Boil lasagna sheets in salted water until just tender, then drain and set aside
    (skip this if using oven-ready sheets).
  3. Prepare the cheese layer: Mix ricotta with a little salt and pepper, or make a béchamel sauce if preferred.
  4. Assemble the lasagna: In a baking dish, spread a thin layer of sauce, add pasta sheets, then layer sauce, ricotta/béchamel, and mozzarella.
    Repeat layers until ingredients are used.
  5. Top and bake: Finish with sauce and a generous layer of mozzarella and Parmesan. Cover with foil and bake at 375°F (190°C) for about 30 minutes,
    then uncover and bake another 10–15 minutes until golden and bubbly.
  6. Rest and serve: Let the lasagna rest for 10–15 minutes before slicing and serving.
